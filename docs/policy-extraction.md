# Policy Extraction — Leave Policy, Altus Group, 29 May 2025

**Source:** "Leave Policy – Altus Group – 29th May 2025" (Issue 1, Revision 1, 9 pages), approved by Mr Ken Simwaba, Managing Director. The signed copy's date line is blank. The PDF is kept outside version control at `docs/policy/altus-leave-policy-2025-05-29.pdf`.

**Statutory reference:** Employment Code Act No. 3 of 2019 (Zambia), checked against the text of the Act on 10 Oct 2026. This is a comparison to help HR, not legal advice; anything marked **Below statute?** should be confirmed by HR and legal counsel.

**Status:** extracted. Leave types are created from this document **inactive**. HR reviews each one in the system and activates it; items marked **HR** must be answered first.

Columns: clause in the policy; what the policy says; how the system treats it; whether HR must confirm; the statutory position.

## Questions for HR (summary)

1. **Annual accrual (A2):** 25 days a year (2.083 a month) or 2.3 a month (27.6 a year)?
2. **Uncommunicated decisions (G10):** confirm escalation instead of automatic approval.
3. **Untaken leave on exit (G4, G13):** the policy contradicts itself, and clause 1.2.5 appears to conflict with s36(5) of the Act.
4. **Carry-forward forfeiture (G6):** may conflict with s36(4).
5. **Sick leave (S3, S4):** is 30 days a cap, or the first stage before 3 months full pay / 3 months half pay as the Act requires?
6. **Family responsibility (F3):** add the 3 days a year required by s40(2)?
7. **Paternity (P1, P4):** working days; and the Act's 12-month service requirement versus none in the policy.
8. **Leave without pay (U1):** calendar or working days; per request or per year.
9. **Recall versus early return (A7, A8):** confirm the different treatment of days.
10. **Maternity under 24 months (X3):** available unpaid, or not available?
11. **Mother's Day (M1):** paid? may it be recorded after the day?
